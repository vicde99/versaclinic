The clinic

A quiet room, a real doctor, and a plan that makes sense.

Versa Aesthetics began in Quezon City with a simple frustration: too many people were being sold treatments they did not need, by people who never looked closely at their skin. We built the opposite — a clinic where a licensed physician assesses you first, explains the options in plain language, and is honest when the answer is "not yet".

Today we care for clients across Metro Manila from our Commonwealth Avenue clinic, with medical-grade technology and a deliberately small team so that every visit feels unhurried. Our aesthetic is restraint: you should look rested and well, never "done".

Assessed by a doctor — not a salesperson. Every plan starts with a clinical skin analysis.

Medical-grade only — FDA-registered devices, authentic products, documented protocols.

Results you can live with — gradual, natural, and matched to your real life and budget.

Dr. Divina Viola Medical Director & Founder

Why Versa

Four reasons clients stay for years.

Aesthetics is a trust business. These are the standards we hold ourselves to, every single appointment.

Doctor-led, always

Your assessment, your plan and your injectables are handled by a licensed physician — never delegated to untrained staff.

Honest recommendations

If a treatment will not help you, we say so. If something can wait six months, we tell you that too. No upselling, ever.

Medical-grade technology

FDA-registered devices and authentic, traceable products. We can show you the certificate for anything we use on you.

Aftercare that continues

You leave with a written plan and a direct line to the clinic. We follow up — because results are built between visits.

The Versa standard

Small details, held consistently.

The things you should be able to expect from any clinic — and rarely can.

Unhurried appointments

Generous time slots so nothing is rushed — including the consultation that comes before any treatment.

Sterile, single-use

Single-use needles and cannulas, hospital-grade sterilisation, and a documented safety protocol for every device.

Progress you can see

Standardised before-and-after photography at every milestone, so improvement is measured — not assumed.

A small, senior team

You see the same faces each visit. Continuity is why your plan actually gets followed through.

Transparent pricing

Quoted before you commit, in writing. Card and e-wallet accepted, with instalment options on programmes.

Real aftercare support

A direct message line to the clinic for the days after treatment — because that is when questions actually come up.
